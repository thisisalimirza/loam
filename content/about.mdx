I grew up across the world, born in Pakistan, and lived in Lebanon, Kenya, and various states within the US. The world around me kept changing, so I became obsessed with what stayed the same: health, community, and the human desire for development. For many years, I was unsure how to commingle these various observations. In undergrad, I floated between studying the classical civilizations of ancient Rome and ancient Greece, biochemistry, game theory, and economics, as well as health policy. I intended on going to medical school, but that plan was punctuated by a brief period where I worked at Epic Systems and co-led one of the company's first collaborative installs & assisted in 2 additional hospital EMR launches. This was followed by a slightly longer stint where I founded and grew a marketing agency to a team of three to five people. Eventually, I received my green card, and my prospects for medical school admission grew significantly.

I was admitted to the University of Connecticut School of Medicine and committed to now entering medical school, surely as a learner, but now as a burgeoning builder as well. To this day, I often have no idea what I am doing. Those who know me know that this is my off-site remark. The ambiguity excites me. Problems excite me. I'm figuring everything out in real time.

This website, I started in 2020 during the pandemic. When, during my classical education on Greece, I realized that much of the folks I was reading had written their greatest works in their twenties, though that is not often known and thought about today. I decided that I was done waiting to act, speak, and share, and I would start now. It has since become an ingrained part of who I am: to reflect, observe, write, and share, and build publicly across social media or this website.
